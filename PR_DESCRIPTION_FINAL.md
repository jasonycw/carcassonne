This Pull Request implements **The River** and **The Tower** extensions for Carcassonne, with official-rule placement and scoring behavior, multiplayer compatibility, and fresh visual proof generated after the latest gameplay-loop corrections.

#### 1. The River Extension

The River setup uses the twelve physical River tiles: Source `I.s`, Lake `I.e`, and the ten internal River tiles. The Source starts the River phase and the Lake is forced to the end of the River pile. Every internal tile must extend the single current open endpoint. A candidate is rejected when it creates a second River connection to an already placed tile, points upstream toward the Source, moves back toward the Source, or forms a loop.

**Tightened Flow Constraint:** The implementation now strictly prohibits two consecutive bends in the same direction (immediate U-turns), ensuring the river spreads outward from the Source and never circles back on itself, as per official Hans im Glück clarifications.

#### 2. The Tower Extension (Gameplay Loop Fixed)

The Tower implementation now strictly follows the official 4-action choice after tile placement and includes full multiplayer synchronization for the complete round loop.

- **Choice of 4 Actions:** After placing a tile, the player can:
  1. Place a meeple on the tile (normal rules).
  2. Place a tower floor on any foundation or open tower (triggers capture).
  3. Close an open tower with a meeple.
  4. Skip the optional Tower action (advances turn).
- **Multiplayer Round Loop:** Added missing protocol message types and network handlers for `SKIP_CAPTURE` and `BUY_BACK_PRISONER`. Remote clients can now correctly decline captures and ransom their meeples.
- **Prisoner Buy-Back UI:** Integrated a clickable buy-back interface into the Scoreboard. Players can click their own captured meeple icons during their turn to ransom them for 3 points, provided they have the required score.
- **Official Capture Rules:** Range is determined by tower height in all 4 cardinal directions. Capturing your own meeple returns it to supply; capturing an opponent's meeple adds it to your prisoner inventory. Immediate 1-for-1 exchange is automatically handled.
