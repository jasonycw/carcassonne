# Official Rules Summary: The River & The Tower

## The River

### Preparation
- **Replace Start Tile**: The River expansion replaces the standard starting tile.
- **Setup**: 
  - Set aside the **Source** and **Lake** tiles.
  - Shuffle the remaining 10 river tiles.
  - Place the Lake tile at the bottom of the river stack.
  - Place the Source tile face-up as the initial starting tile.
- **Gameplay Flow**: Players must draw from the river stack first until it is exhausted before drawing normal tiles.

### Placement Rules
- **Continuity**: Each river tile must continue the river.
- **No U-Turns**: Immediate 180-degree turns are forbidden. Specifically, two consecutive bends cannot turn in the same direction if it results in a U-turn back toward the source.
- **No Looping**: The river cannot flow back into itself.
- **Meeples**: 
  - Meeples can be placed on features (cities, roads, cloisters, farms) on river tiles following normal rules.
  - Meeples **cannot** be placed on the river itself.
  - Scoring for features on river tiles is identical to the base game.

## The Tower

### Components & Setup
- **Tower Floors**: 30 floors total.
- **Supply per Player**:
  - 2 players: 10 floors
  - 3 players: 9 floors
  - 4 players: 7 floors
  - 5 players: 6 floors
  - 6 players: 5 floors
- **Tower Foundations**: 18 tiles with tower foundation spaces.

### Gameplay Actions
After placing a tile, a player has four options:
1. **Place a Meeple**: Standard rules on the tile just placed.
2. **Place a Tower Floor (Action A/B)**:
   - On a tower foundation of any tile already on the board.
   - On an existing open tower.
   - **Capture**: Placing a floor allows capturing **one meeple** within range.
3. **Place a Meeple on a Tower (Action C)**:
   - Place a meeple on top of an open tower to **close** it.
   - Closed towers cannot be extended.
   - The meeple stays until the end of the game or until captured.

### Capture & Range
- **Range**: A tower of height *H* can capture a meeple on any tile within *H* steps in the 4 cardinal directions (orthogonal).
- **Prisoners**:
  - Captured meeples are placed in front of the capturer.
  - **Automatic Exchange**: If two players have each other's meeples, they are exchanged immediately.
  - **Ransom**: A player can pay **3 points** to buy back one of their captured meeples during their turn.

### Scoring
- The Tower does not change scoring rules for features.
- Buy-back costs 3 points (capturer +3, owner -3). Points cannot go below 0.
