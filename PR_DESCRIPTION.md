This Pull Request implements **The River** and **The Tower** extensions for Carcassonne, with official-rule placement and scoring behavior, multiplayer compatibility, and fresh visual proof generated after the latest River-flow correction.

#### 1. The River Extension

The River setup uses the twelve physical River tiles: Source `I.s`, Lake `I.e`, and the ten internal River tiles. The Source starts the River phase and the Lake is forced to the end of the River pile. Every internal tile must extend the single current open endpoint. A candidate is rejected when it creates a second River connection to an already placed tile, points upstream toward the Source, moves back toward the Source, or forms a loop. After the Lake is placed, the game exits the River phase and resumes the normal tile pile.

The native asset audit is reflected in `TileData.js`. In particular, `I.s` exits East; `I.e` receives the River from the West; `CcII` has a South-East River bend with city covering the North and West; `LIRI` has a straight West-East River, a North farm containing the cathedral, and a road running from the cathedral to the South; `CIRI` has a West-East River, North city, and South road; `RIrI` has a North-South River crossed by a West-East road; and `RrII` has a West-South River bend with a North-East road.

#### 2. The Tower Extension

The Tower implementation supports placing floors on eligible tower foundations, capturing meeples in orthogonal range according to tower height, capturing the active player's own meeples, exchanging opposing prisoners, buying back a prisoner for three points, and closing a tower with a meeple. Tower actions are synchronized through the multiplayer game state and the normal turn flow.
